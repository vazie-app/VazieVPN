## What you may do

- Build the app from this repository for yourself, change it and share your changes under the GPL.
- Say truthfully that your work is "based on Vazie VPN" or "a fork of Vazie VPN".
- Show the unmodified Vazie VPN icon when you write about Vazie VPN itself.

## What a fork or redistributed build must do

A build you distribute — in an app store, on a website or anywhere else — that is not the official one published
from [github.com/vazie-app/VazieVPN](https://github.com/vazie-app/VazieVPN):

- uses its own name, not "Vazie" or "Vazie VPN", nor a confusingly similar one;
- replaces the app icons and the store graphics with its own;
- uses its own application ID (not `app.vazie.vpn`) and its own signing key;
- does not point at Vazie's servers or website (`vazie.api.url`, `vazie.site.url`) unless Vazovsky agrees;
- does not suggest that Vazovsky or Vazie made, endorses or supports it.
