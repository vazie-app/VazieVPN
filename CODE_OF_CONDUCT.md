# Code of Conduct

Vazie VPN is a small project, and the people around it — users, reporters and contributors — deserve the same
respect the code tries to show its users.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient. Many people here write in a second language.
- Assume good intent, and discuss the change, not the person.
- Keep issues and reviews on topic, and accept that the maintainer decides what fits the project.
- Respect privacy: never post anyone's configuration, subscription link, IP address or personal details.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats, discrimination or sexualised language and imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Spam, advertising, or promotion of servers, VPN services or other paid offers.
- Asking for, sharing or reselling access to servers or configurations.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block participants who do not
follow this code.

Report a problem privately to **support@vazie.app** with "Code of Conduct" in the subject. Reports are handled
confidentially.

This code applies in the repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and wherever someone represents the
project.
