Vazie VPN is a VPN client for the VLESS protocol. It connects your phone to a server you already have — your own or your provider's — through an encrypted tunnel. The app comes with no servers of its own.

YOUR OWN CONFIGURATIONS
• Add a VLESS link by pasting it from the clipboard.
• Or a subscription link: Vazie VPN adds every configuration in it at once.
• REALITY and TLS, over TCP, WebSocket and gRPC.
• Configurations stay on the device, encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore. They are never uploaded.
• The latency to each server is shown in the list.

CONNECTION
• Connect and disconnect with one tap; a notification shows the tunnel's state and has a disconnect button.
• Split tunneling: send every app through the VPN, every app except the ones you choose, or only the ones you choose.

ALWAYS AT HAND
• Two home-screen widgets, in light and dark.
• A Quick Settings tile.
• Your recent configurations as app shortcuts.

ABOUT DATA
• No account and no sign-up.
• No ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting or tracking libraries.
• No log of the sites you visit.

DESIGN
Two themes — Milk and Night Indigo — ten app icons, English and Russian.

A VPN changes who can see your traffic; it does not make you anonymous.
